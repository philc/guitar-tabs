    Eadd9 : 0-11-9-11-0-0
    A6add9: X- 0-9-11-0-0

    A     : 577600 (Same as Badd4, slid down
    E     : 079900 (Similar to Badd4, down one string)

    Eadd9 : 0-11-9-11-0-0
